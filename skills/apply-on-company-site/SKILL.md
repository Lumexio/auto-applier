---
name: apply-on-company-site
description: "A job board page that hands you over to the employer's own careers site or application system."
when: ["apply (on|at|via|through) (the )?(company|employer|careers)", "external (application|site)", "you will be redirected", "continue to (the )?(company|employer)"]
---
1. Choose the button or link that goes to the employer's own site or its application system (Greenhouse, Lever, Workday, Ashby, SmartRecruiters).
2. Never choose "Save", "Share", "Report", "Similar jobs" or a link to another job board.
3. If the only entries leave for a site you do not recognise as an employer or application system, choose stop.
